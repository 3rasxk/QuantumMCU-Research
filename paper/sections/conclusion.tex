\section{Conclusion}
Memory ends \hall, \bell\ and \qft\ at $n=\MemWallUno$, \MemWallEspLo\ and \MemWallEspHi\ on the Uno, \espA\ and \espB, while \grover\ on the ESP boards stopped at the per-point time cap before any wall. The memory limit is consistent with one low-$n$ read (largest free block on the \espB, a predictor chosen after the walls were known; the Uno check is close to circular); held-out time is predicted to about \MapeTwoClean\,\% on the scored points (not \grover\ on the ESP boards, not identifiable on the Uno) with a model that is a fit, not a gate cost; and offload overtakes local execution within brackets of measured $n$ that depend on the host PC. The results rest on one unit per board, one PC and one baud rate, and several analysis rules were set after the data were seen.
