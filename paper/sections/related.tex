\section{Related Work}
Dense statevector simulators and their exponential memory demand are well documented~\cite{haener2017}, as are GPU and CPU simulators such as Qiskit Aer (cited through the Qiskit paper~\cite{javadiabhari2024}; Aer has no paper of its own) and recent fast statevector simulators that trade numerical precision against memory and speed~\cite{yusufov2023}. Statevector simulation on a microcontroller has been shown for a few qubits on an RP2040 board~\cite{carney2023}; a limited literature search found no peer-reviewed study on Arduino or ESP boards, and timing measurement on microcontrollers with an external reference is common practice~\cite{TODO-mcu-timing}. The circuits we use are textbook constructions (QFT~\cite{coppersmith1994}, Grover search~\cite{grover1996}). We do not claim novelty over this literature.
